# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want Moonlit Map Adventure to be a welcoming and respectful project for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or point of view.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and kindness.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus on the issue or contribution, not the person.
- Assume good intent while addressing misunderstandings openly.
- Respect different experiences and perspectives.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not welcome:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Threats, intimidation, or deliberate disruption.
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, insulting, or persistently derailing discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers through the repository's available contact or reporting channels. Please include what happened, when it occurred, and any relevant links or evidence.

Reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and activities, including the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related events.
